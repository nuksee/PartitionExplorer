# PartitionExplorer privacy policy

PartitionExplorer does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- It reads file and folder names and sizes on your PC only to show them to you. Nothing leaves your computer.
- It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads, and it makes no network connections.
- Its settings, such as window size and the last folder you opened, are saved locally in your Windows user profile.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nuksee/PartitionExplorer/issues.
